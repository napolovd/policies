Privacy Policy

Last updated: Nov 15, 2026

Welcome to the privacy policy of CatTorrent, developed by Pirate Cats. Your privacy is important to us. This document outlines how we handle your information.

Data Collection

CatTorrent has no accounts and no servers of its own, and it does not collect any personal information by itself. The torrents you add, their names, and the files you download stay on your device. They are never included in analytics or crash reports.

The BitTorrent Network

CatTorrent is a BitTorrent client. When it downloads or shares a torrent, your device's IP address and the torrent you are downloading are visible to the trackers, the DHT network and the other peers taking part in that torrent, as with any BitTorrent client. This is how the protocol works, and that information is not sent to us.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, which may collect information as described in their respective privacy policies:

  • Firebase Analytics: Used to understand how the app is used, through events such as a torrent being added or finished. The events carry no torrent names, file names or torrent identifiers. Firebase may record in-app purchases. For details, visit Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Used to collect crash reports so we can fix them. A report contains technical information about the crash and the device (such as the device model and Android version) and the app's most recent log lines, with file and torrent names removed. For details, visit Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • AdMob: Used for serving ads. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and IP address, including to estimate your approximate location. For details, visit AdMob Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing: Used to sell the optional "Remove ads" purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details; the app only learns whether you own the purchase. For details, visit Google Privacy Policy.

Your Consent Choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing ads or collecting analytics. If you do not consent, analytics collection is switched off and ads are not personalized. You can change your choice at any time from the app's menu. Buying "Remove ads" stops ads entirely.

Permissions

  • Storage folders: Only the folders you choose, used to save the files you download.
  • Notifications and background service: Used to keep downloads running while the app is in the background, and to show their progress.
  • Network: Used to download and share torrents.

Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information from our servers.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@cattorrent.com.
